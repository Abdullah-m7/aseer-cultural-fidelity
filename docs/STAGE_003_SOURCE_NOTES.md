# Stage 003 Source Notes

Stage 003 deliberately adds sources that broaden the testbed from culinary heritage to tourism-facing visual art, architecture, village interpretation, destination strategy, and authenticity.

## Source-selection principle

Held-out cases prefer primary or official institutional sources where possible. Source anchors are short paraphrases rather than copied passages.

### UNESCO — Al-Qatt Al-Asiri

Used for regional identity, the documented role of women, interior-wall practice, guest-room context, and the 2017 intangible-heritage inscription.

### Saudipedia — Handicrafts in Aseer Region

Used for honey production as an inherited Aseer craft, its ecological basis, and the coexistence of heritage with contemporary value-added products.

### Aseer Development Authority — Aseer Villages

Used for local-material architecture, relationship to landscape, and the region’s cultural/aesthetic identity.

### Aseer Development Authority — Al-Quriyyah Village

Used for a place-specific concierge case: stone-and-clay houses, locally named `Al-Masareeb` passageways, and the ancient village well.

### Aseer Development Authority — Strategy / From Aseer

Used for official destination-positioning and authenticity claims: year-round tourism, authentic culture, diverse nature, community participation, tangible heritage, and intangible heritage.

## Important limitation

Official sources can themselves simplify or institutionalize living culture. Expert calibration therefore tests not only the invariant wording but also whether a source is sufficient for the cultural claim being encoded.
