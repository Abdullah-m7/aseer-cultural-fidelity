# Stage 001 Report — Source-Anchored Pilot Foundation

**Branch:** `research/stage-001-source-anchored-pilot`  
**Stage objective:** move from networking idea to falsifiable research instrument.

## Decision

The project will **not** compete head-on with SaudiCulture or Saudi-Alignment as another broad Saudi cultural knowledge benchmark.

Instead, Stage 001 locks a more specific problem:

> measuring cultural distortion induced by applied generative transformations.

This keeps the Aseer testbed scientifically useful while allowing future expansion to other regions and non-culinary domains.

## Evidence reviewed

The initial literature scan found mature adjacent work on cultural values, cultural knowledge, MENA alignment, Saudi cultural competence, and cross-lingual shifts. It also found strong institutional justification for an Aseer hospitality/gastronomy testbed, including:

- Aseer's World Region of Gastronomy recognition;
- documented Aseer food heritage;
- King Khalid University's Food Gifts initiative linking innovation to preservation of Aseer cultural identity;
- tourism/hospitality partnerships and applied research activity at the College of Tourism and Hospitality.

## Scientific risk identified

The phrase **cultural fidelity** is too broad if left undefined. The project therefore anchors judgments to case-specific evidence and uses critical distortion as the primary endpoint.

The main risk remains expert validity: an official source can document a claim, but it may still oversimplify living regional practice. Domain experts should therefore validate invariants before the full benchmark is frozen.

## Stage 002 entry criteria

Stage 002 should begin only after:

1. all pilot cases pass schema validation;
2. sources are reviewed for authority and ambiguity;
3. at least one small model run is completed across neutral/fidelity-aware conditions;
4. annotation disagreements are examined;
5. cases that merely test generic factuality are removed or rewritten to test transformation-specific drift.

## Collaboration-ready artifact

The project becomes suitable for expert outreach when it can show a one-page brief containing:

- the research question;
- 3–5 example transformations;
- the invariant/distortion framework;
- early pilot results;
- a specific expert request: validate whether the proposed invariants and unacceptable transformations reflect the domain accurately.

That is a materially stronger collaboration invitation than asking for general feedback on an idea.
